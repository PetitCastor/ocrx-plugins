# Third-party notices — Item Tracker

## Aurelius-Draco/sc-inventory-tracker (MIT)

The freight-total signal — reading a station's item count from the cursor of the last page of the
Freight Inventory Grid, and keeping a floor from pages that have a next page so a searched or
filtered list is not taken for the total — and the idea of checking a log ledger against what the
game shows on screen, come from sc-inventory-tracker v3.0 by Aurelius-Draco. The ideas and the shape
of the log lines were reused; no code was copied.
